## **🔹 1\. Brand Overview**

### **Brand Name:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Brand Name]`  
* **Definition:** The official name of the brand.

### **Tagline/Slogan:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Tagline/Slogan]`  
* **Definition:** A short, memorable phrase that captures the essence of the brand.  
* **Example:** "Sustainably Yours," "Transform Your Fitness Journey," "Innovate Your World."

### **Mission Statement:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Mission Statement]`  
* **Definition:** A concise statement outlining the brand’s purpose and primary objectives.  
* **Example:** "To provide eco-friendly products that enhance everyday living while protecting our planet."

### **Vision Statement:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Vision Statement]`  
* **Definition:** A forward-looking statement describing the brand’s long-term goals and aspirations.  
* **Example:** "To be the leading provider of sustainable solutions worldwide, inspiring a greener future."

### **Core Values:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Core Values]`  
* **Definition:** Fundamental beliefs and guiding principles that drive the brand’s actions and decisions.  
* **Example:** "Sustainability, Innovation, Customer-Centricity, Integrity."

### **Brand Personality:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Brand Personality]`  
* **Definition:** The set of human characteristics attributed to the brand, shaping how it is perceived.  
* **Example:** "Friendly, Innovative, Trustworthy, Energetic."

### **Brand Voice:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Brand Voice]`  
* **Definition:** The consistent tone and style used in all brand communications.  
* **Example:** "Conversational and approachable with a focus on empowerment and positivity."

## **🔹 3\. Brand Messaging**

### **Key Messages:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Key Message 1], [Key Message 2], [Key Message 3]`  
* **Definition:** Core messages that communicate the brand’s value and differentiators.  
* **Example:**  
  * **Key Message 1:** "Our products are made from 100% recycled materials."  
  * **Key Message 2:** "We innovate to create solutions that make your life easier and greener."  
  * **Key Message 3:** "Join our community in making a positive impact on the environment."

### **Unique Selling Proposition (USP):**

* **Placeholder:** `[USP]`  
* **Definition:** The unique benefit that sets the brand apart from competitors.  
* **Example:** "EcoWare offers the only reusable water bottles that come with a lifetime warranty and a recycling program."

### **Elevator Pitch:**

* **Placeholder:** `[Elevator Pitch]`  
* **Definition:** A brief, persuasive speech that succinctly explains what the brand does, who it serves, and what makes it unique.  
* **Example:** "At EcoWare, we provide sustainable, high-quality water bottles that help reduce plastic waste. Our innovative designs and commitment to the environment make us the preferred choice for eco-conscious consumers."

## **🔹 14\. Media Prompt Instructions**

### **How to Define Media Prompts:**

When defining media prompts, provide clear and detailed descriptions to ensure the LLM agent can generate accurate and high-quality content. Each media type may require specific information to achieve the desired outcome.

* **Image Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Describe the scene, subjects, colors, style, and any specific elements you want to include in the image.  
  * **Example:** "A vibrant image of young professionals using eco-friendly water bottles in a modern office setting, with a focus on sustainability and collaboration."  
* **Audio Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Specify the tone, style, and purpose of the audio content. Include any specific phrases or keywords that should be included.  
  * **Example:** "A friendly and enthusiastic voiceover explaining the features of our new eco-friendly water bottle, emphasizing sustainability and durability."  
* **Video Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Outline the key scenes, actions, and messages. Include details about the setting, characters, and visual style.  
  * **Example:** "A short animated video showcasing the key benefits of our AI-powered software, including increased efficiency and user-friendly interface, with engaging graphics and upbeat background music."  
* **Upscale Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Provide details about the current quality of the media and the desired improvements. Mention specific aspects that need enhancement.  
  * **Example:** "Enhance the resolution of our product images to 4K quality without losing detail, focusing on the texture and color accuracy of the eco-friendly materials."  
* **GIF Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Describe the action or message the GIF should convey. Specify any text or animations required.  
  * **Example:** "A looping GIF showing the transformation process of our product from raw materials to finished eco-friendly water bottle, with the text 'Sustainable from Start to Finish.'"  
* **Animation Prompt:**  
  * **Instruction:** Outline the narrative or educational content of the animation. Include details about characters, movements, and key points.  
  * **Example:** "An animated explainer video detailing how our software integrates with existing systems to improve productivity, featuring a friendly mascot guiding users through the process."  
* **Other Media Prompts:**  
  * **Instruction:** Provide a clear description of the additional media content needed, specifying the type, purpose, and key elements.  
  * **Example:** "A downloadable PDF guide on using our product effectively, including step-by-step instructions, tips, and high-quality images to illustrate each step."
